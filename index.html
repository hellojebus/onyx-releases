Your files. Your format. Just faster.

Onyx adds structure on top of plain markdown — never instead of it.

Plain markdown, always

Open your vault in any editor and nothing is lost. Onyx writes metadata under a tidy onyx: key — your own frontmatter is never touched.

First-class checkboxes

Every - [ ] gets a stable identity, metadata, and queryable state. Tasks become real objects, not just text.

Today / Week / Overdue

Due dates roll up into instant agenda views — under 50 ms no matter how big the vault gets, thanks to the SQLite sidecar.

Wikilinks & backlinks

Obsidian-style [[wikilinks]] connect files, headings, and individual tasks — with a backlinks panel to walk them in reverse.

Automatic archival

Finished tasks tuck themselves into a per-project .completed.md with a timestamp and their original heading path. Source always wins on conflict.

Local-first & private

Everything lives on your disk. No account, no cloud, no telemetry. A native Tauri build — roughly 10 MB and ~80 MB of RAM, not a browser in disguise.
