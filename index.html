Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Notes and todos in one place, with the fiddly parts already decided for you.

Notes and tasks together

Write a note, drop a task into it, check it off when it's done. Your thoughts and your to-dos live side by side — it feels like jotting on paper, only it never gets lost.

Knows what's due

Add a due date and Onyx keeps track. One glance shows what's due today, what's coming up, and what you let slip — across everything.

Separate Spaces for everything

Give work, home, and that side project their own Space. Each one has its own projects and lists, and you switch between them with a single click — so things never bleed together.

Link things together

Mention another note by name and it turns into a link. Handy for connecting a task or note to the person, project, or place it belongs to — no thinking required.

Tidies up after you

Finished tasks quietly move out of the way once they're done, so your list stays focused on what's left. Nothing is deleted — it's all still there if you go looking.

Yours, and only yours

Everything stays on your Mac. No account to make, no cloud to trust, nothing tracked. It opens fast, stays out of your way, and your notes and todos are just files you own forever.

Fully customizable

Your Space, your way.

Clients and invoices for work, courses and assignments for school, books and trips for personal — create a type for whatever you keep track of, and Onyx treats each one as its own thing.
